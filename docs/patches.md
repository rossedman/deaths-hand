# Six evil patches

Rendered in `test/out/ex*.wav` by the same engine and the same panel mapping the firmware runs. Knob positions are clock faces: 7:00 is fully counter-clockwise, 12:00 is centre, 5:00 is fully clockwise. "S1"–"S4" means hold the button and turn knob 1–4. Routing is the number of taps after power-on. Untouched shift knobs stay at their defaults: ratio 1:1, sine, symmetry and fine at noon.

## 1 · Reactor hum

A low drone whose feedback breathes in and out.

Toggle up, routing 0. Pitch 8:40, FLESH 10:30, LEGION 8:10, S1 8:20 (ratio ½), S3 1:30. Rock **MALICE slowly between 8:00 and 12:00**.

## 2 · Lorenz growl

A chaotic modulator in audio range, like an engine that won't idle.

**Toggle down**, routing 1. Pitch 9:30, S1 1:10, S2 4:00 (Lorenz). Ease **FLESH from 11:00 to 1:00**.

## 3 · Chaos bells

Inharmonic struck metal that gets more unhinged as it goes.

Toggle up, routing 2. Pitch 12:00, FLESH 7:30, S1 12:00 (ratio 3). Sequencer into **CV_5**, a fast decaying envelope (0–2.5 V) into **CV_6** (FLESH), trigger both together. Turn **MALICE from 11:30 to 3:30**.

## 4 · Feedback scream

The pitch dives while feedback tears into noise.

Toggle up, routing 0. FLESH 1:00, S1 10:10 (ratio 1.5), S3 10:00. Together, sweep **pitch from 4:10 to 9:30** and **MALICE from 12:00 to 5:00**.

## 5 · Possession

Everything rising, then the modulator turns chaotic.

Toggle up, routing 3. Pitch 10:20, LEGION 10:00, S1 11:05 (ratio 2), S2 2:00 (square), S3 2:30. Sweep **FLESH from 11:30 to 4:00** and **MALICE from 9:30 to 3:30**, then **flip S2 to 4:00** (Lorenz) halfway.

## 6 · Legion

One voice becomes a swarm while the fold opens.

Toggle up, routing 0. Pitch 8:40, MALICE 9:00. Sweep **LEGION from 8:30 to 5:00** and **FLESH from 10:00 to 1:00**.
