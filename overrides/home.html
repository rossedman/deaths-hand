The voice

Two voices.
One devours.

A modulator bends the primary through through-zero FM, folds it, and rings it. Then three things make it misbehave: the primary feeds its own phase, it feeds the modulator's pitch, and the modulator can become a strange attractor that never repeats.

  • {% include ".icons/material/sine-wave.svg" %} Linear through-zero FM: the phase runs backwards
  • {% include ".icons/material/sync.svg" %} Self-feedback from pure sine to saw to noise

The macros

Three knobs.
Three sins.

Each macro sweeps several parameters along its own curve, so one knob, or one CV, tells one story. The host tests hold each to it.

feedback, then chaos

The panel

No words.
Only sigils.

The top row is the voice: pitch and three character macros. Each sigil repeats on the jack that drives it, so the panel reads in columns. Bone rings are inputs; red rings are outputs, and they sit on the altar.

Three vessels

One core.
Three bodies.

The DSP lives in dsp/: no heap, no platform headers, volts in and volts out. The firmware, the plugin and the tests include it unchanged.

patch.Init

Eurorack hardware

Pitch and the three macros up front, their CVs on the jacks. Hold the button for ratio, shape, symmetry and fine, with soft takeover so nothing jumps.

make firmware
make -C hw program-dfu

VCV Rack

16 HP, the same panel

Mirrors the hardware knob for knob, through the same panel mapping, so a patch moves between them unchanged.

make vcv RACK_DIR=../Rack-SDK

Host tests
